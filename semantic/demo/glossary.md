# Glossary

Business terms inferred from the schema. All `proposed`.

## Customer

A party that places orders, stored in `customers`. See [entities/customers.md](entities/customers.md).

## Order

A purchase made by a customer, stored in `orders`. See [entities/orders.md](entities/orders.md).

## Segment

A classification of a customer, stored in `customers.segment`. Observed values: `Enterprise`, `SMB`, `Consumer`.

## Revenue

Money from completed orders; see [metrics.md](metrics.md). Requires review — see open questions there.
