# Glossary

All definitions are `proposed` and require human review.

## Retail Banking

- **AML (Anti-Money Laundering):** Regulatory process to detect and prevent money laundering; alerts indicate potential suspicious activity.
- **Arrears:** Amount or days overdue on loan repayments.
- **DORMANT:** Customer account/relationship with no recent activity (business rule to confirm).
- **DECEASED:** Deceased customer status; typically excluded from active customer counts.
- **Home loan balance:** Outstanding principal on home loan; may be NULL if no home loan.
- **LVR (Loan-to-Value Ratio):** Ratio of loan amount to property value, expressed as percentage.
- **MASS / AFFLUENT / PRIVATE:** Retail customer segments.
- **PII (Personally Identifiable Information):** Data that identifies an individual (e.g. names, emails, phone numbers, dates of birth).

## Commercial Banking

- **Borrower:** Business entity with lending facilities.
- **Collateral:** Assets securing a loan/facility.
- **Collateral cover ratio:** Measure of collateral relative to exposure.
- **Covenant:** Loan condition/requirement; breaches indicate non-compliance.
- **Facility:** Lending arrangement (e.g. term loan, overdraft, revolving credit).
- **High-risk borrower:** Borrower classified as high risk based on risk grade/band.
- **PD (Probability of Default):** Estimated likelihood of default, usually as percentage.
- **Relationship Manager:** Bank staff responsible for the business relationship.
- **Risk grade/band:** Credit risk rating scale (1-10; bands like LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH/WATCHLIST).

## Insurance

- **Catastrophe exposure:** Estimated exposure to natural/man-made catastrophic perils.
- **Earned premium:** Premium earned for coverage provided over a period.
- **IBNR:** Incurred But Not Reported claims (provision for claims not yet reported).
- **Incurred claims:** Claims cost including paid and reserves (incurred = paid + reserve in this context).
- **Loss ratio:** (Incurred claims / Earned premium) × 100%.
- **Peril:** Cause of loss (e.g. fire, flood, collision, burglary).
- **Product line:** Broad insurance category (e.g. HOME, MOTOR).
- **Reserve:** Amount set aside for outstanding claim costs.
- **Sum insured:** Maximum amount payable under policy.
